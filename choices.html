Choices & Pressure

Bust the myths, learn what to say, and practice before the moment comes.

Myth or fact? Tap a card

Ways to say no

You do not need a perfect speech. Keep it short, calm and confident.

Say it simply

"No thanks." "Not for me." "I'm good." Repeat it if needed. You do not have to explain.

Offer something else

"Let's get food instead." "Want to play something?" Giving others a better idea often ends the pressure.

Blame a rule

"My coach does random checks." "My family would find out." An outside reason can be easier than a personal one.

Leave

"I'm heading out." If you feel unsafe or pushed, go. Text someone you trust or call a family member to pick you up.

Have a plan before you need it Agree on a code word with a parent or friend, for example a text that means "please come get me, no questions asked."

Try it: Pressure Simulator

Four real-life situations. Pick what you would do and see how it plays out. There is no penalty for trying the other options afterwards.
