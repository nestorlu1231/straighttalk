Fact Files

What common substances do to the body and mind, and why they are risky. Information only, no how-to.

Showing 8 fact files.

Alcohol

Depressant

What it does

Slows the brain and body, which affects judgment, balance and reaction time.

Short-term risks

Slurred speech, poor decisions, blackouts, accidents and injuries, and alcohol poisoning when too much is taken.

Longer-term risks

Liver damage, dependence, mood problems, and harm to a developing teen brain.

Nicotine: cigarettes & vapes

Stimulant

What it does

Nicotine speeds up the heart and quickly creates dependence. Many vapes contain it.

Short-term risks

Raised heart rate, irritated throat and lungs, and cravings that make it hard to concentrate.

Longer-term risks

Strong addiction, lung and heart problems, and effects on attention and learning in young people. "Safer than cigarettes" does not mean safe.

Methamphetamine ("shabu")

Stimulant · Illegal

What it does

A powerful stimulant that overloads the brain's reward system and pushes the heart and body into overdrive.

Short-term risks

Very high heart rate and blood pressure, overheating, severe anxiety, no sleep, and risky or violent behavior.

Longer-term risks

Strong dependence, paranoia and hallucinations, heart and dental damage, memory and mood problems.

Cannabis

Mind-altering · Illegal for recreation in the PH

What it does

Changes mood, perception and thinking, and slows reaction time.

Short-term risks

Poor memory and focus, anxiety or panic in some people, and impaired coordination that makes accidents more likely.

Longer-term risks

Trouble with learning and memory when used regularly by teens, and dependence for some people.

Opioids & misused painkillers

Depressant

What it does

Blocks pain and slows breathing. Doctors use some opioids for severe pain, but misuse is very dangerous.

Short-term risks

Drowsiness, confusion, and dangerously slow breathing that can be fatal, especially when mixed with alcohol or other sedatives.

Longer-term risks

Rapid tolerance, severe dependence and painful withdrawal.

Misused prescription medicines

Other risks

What it does

Sleeping pills, sedatives, ADHD or pain medicines are made for a specific person and condition. Using someone else's or using more than prescribed changes how they act.

Short-term risks

Unpredictable reactions, overdose, and dangerous interactions with alcohol or other medicines.

Longer-term risks

Dependence and health problems. Only take medicine as directed for you by a doctor or pharmacist.

Inhalants

Other risks

What it does

Everyday products such as solvents, glues or sprays are not made to be breathed in. Their fumes quickly affect the brain.

Short-term risks

Dizziness, confusion, and sudden death from heart or breathing problems, even the very first time.

Longer-term risks

Damage to the brain, lungs, liver and kidneys, and memory problems.

Hallucinogens & "party drugs"

Mind-altering · Illegal

What it does

Distorts perception, thoughts and sense of time, and can raise body temperature and heart rate.

Short-term risks

Panic, confusion, dangerous behavior, overheating and dehydration. Pills and powders sold on the street can contain unknown substances.

Longer-term risks

Lasting anxiety or mood problems for some people, and harm from repeated use.

Mixing is extra risky Combining substances, including alcohol with medicines, can multiply the dangers in ways that are hard to predict.
